The three badges

One question. Three clear answers.

What role did humans and artificial intelligence really play in the final result? Each badge answers that question, without judging the value or quality of the work.

Green accent

Human production

The human creates.

The creative content is conceived and made by one or more people. Tools may help, without generating the final content themselves.

  • Writing an article yourself
  • Drawing on a graphics tablet
  • Fixing spelling, adjusting colours, stabilizing a video
Blue accent

AI-assisted production

The human creates with AI.

Both the human and AI contribute to the final content, and the human makes a substantial creative contribution.

  • Rewriting half of a first draft and adding your own ideas
  • Composing one image from several generations
  • Directing the script, pacing and editing of a video
Purple accent

AI-generated production

The human asks, AI creates.

AI produces most of the creative content from a human request, even a detailed one, with at most minor touch-ups.

  • An AI-produced image used almost as is
  • A generated text, barely edited
  • Picking the best of several versions
In context

A badge, right where the public sees it.

At the end of a video, under an article or on the back of a book: the badge sits near the creation, at a readable size, with a short note when needed.

The two-question test

Find the right badge in under a minute.

Two questions are enough in the vast majority of cases. Answer for the creation as the public sees, reads or hears it.

Question 1 of 2

Did artificial intelligence generate, write or creatively transform any part of the final content?

Technical functions (fixing spelling, removing noise, stabilizing, cropping, converting a format) do not count.

Question 2 of 2

Did the human make a substantial creative contribution to the final result?

Writing, rewriting, transforming, assembling, creative editing, or precise and continuous direction that shapes the meaning, structure or final form. Choosing, rejecting or repeating prompts is not enough.

Your badge

The check test
If you removed everything AI actually created, would a substantial human creation remain?

If yes: AI-assisted production. If almost everything disappears: AI-generated production.

When in doubt

In case of reasonable doubt, choose the badge that informs the public most clearly about AI’s real involvement. Between human and assisted: assisted. Between assisted and generated: generated.

The question that really matters

“If the person viewing this creation knew how it was made, would they find the badge fair and true to reality?”

The principles

What matters isn’t whether AI was used. It’s who shaped the result.

The framework rests on a few simple ideas, designed to last despite how quickly the tools evolve.

Who shaped the final content?

The right badge depends on the real nature of each contribution, not simply on whether an AI tool was present.

Directing isn’t always shaping

Giving a topic or choosing between versions is directing. Direction becomes creative when it determines the final form in a precise and continuous way.

Technical AI isn’t creative AI

Removing noise, stabilizing, fixing spelling: these functions don’t change the badge. Inventing, writing, drawing or composing do.

No arbitrary percentages

No rule like “30% human.” Such figures would be impossible to measure and would give a false sense of precision.

The number of prompts doesn’t count

One prompt followed by a deep human transformation: assisted. A hundred prompts without ever touching the content: generated.

A good-faith self-declaration

The person who publishes chooses the badge, because they know how the work was made. They commit to correcting it if it proves inaccurate.

The complementary note

For a mixed creation where AI plays an important creative role, a short note explains who did what.

AI-generated images, human script and editing. Human text, AI-generated voice. AI-generated illustration, human text.

✓Do

  • Keep the round shape, gold rim and navy blue
  • Keep the full labels intact
  • Place the badge near the title, caption or credits
  • Provide alt text, and a caption for the mini version
  • Use one badge per creation when the modes differ

✕Don’t

  • Swap the colours (green, blue, purple)
  • Distort, rotate, crop or make the badge transparent
  • Add laurels, medals, seals or a publisher’s signature
  • Suggest an official certification or audit
  • Shorten “assisted” or “generated”

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Is this a certification?

No. The badge is a self-declaration chosen by the person or organization that publishes. No outside body grants it. It is a good-faith commitment to the public.

Does a “human” badge mean “better”?

No. Human doesn’t mean better, AI-assisted doesn’t mean less authentic, and AI-generated doesn’t mean without human involvement. The badges are neither a ranking nor an award.

Can I put a single badge on my whole website?

A badge describes one specific creation. If a website, book or channel brings together creations made in different ways, each one carries its own badge.

What if my creation changes after it’s published?

If it is significantly modified, review its badge. An inaccurate badge is corrected promptly, and visibly when the error was significant.

Do the badges replace legal requirements?

No. Some laws require specific notices for content created with AI. They vary by country and type of content. When a law requires a notice, it is added to the badge.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The badges and the framework can be downloaded free of charge, with no sign-up, to be used according to the guidelines.
